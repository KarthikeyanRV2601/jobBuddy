# JobBuddy company directory

Reviewed: 19 November 2026. Scope: India engineering opportunities and remote jobs whose listings explicitly permit India. This is a curated, expandable directory, not an exhaustive list or a salary ranking. Company inclusion does not guarantee an opening, compensation amount, or India eligibility.

## Research basis

- Primary pay research: [India software-engineer leaderboard](https://www.levels.fyi/leaderboard/Software-Engineer/All-Levels/country/India/) and [India senior-engineer leaderboard](https://www.levels.fyi/leaderboard/Software-Engineer/Senior-Engineer/country/India/). These report total compensation across submitted salaries; they are not job-offer guarantees.
- Additional product targets have compensation-profile links for further research and are explicitly distinguished from leaderboard-backed entries. Sanofi is retained from the existing selection.
- Career destinations were checked against official career pages or their public employer job boards. Protected sites that block automated requests remain direct career links.
- Product companies means established product businesses. Startups & scaleups is an editorial growth-stage grouping, not a claim about private ownership, IPO status, or legal company classification.

## Search coverage

**74 companies: 46 product companies and 28 startups/scaleups. 44 automatic retrieval configurations and 30 official career-site links.**

Automatic integrations were checked using live public responses. Supported connectors include the original Oracle, Microsoft and Sanofi integrations plus Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Eightfold and Amazon Jobs. Some feeds provide only titles, departments and locations; match evidence is labeled accordingly. Career-site links do not return fake zero-result searches.

All selections remain available in the compact scrollable picker. Name search, category tabs, Auto-only filtering, Select shown and Clear do not trigger network requests. Search runs on button click in batches of four companies, displays results progressively and can be stopped without losing completed batches.

Full-board responses are cached for five minutes and reused for additional keywords/pages. Provider errors retain other companies’ results. Adding a company requires a catalog entry and a validated connector; entries without a connector explicitly use direct career-site mode.
